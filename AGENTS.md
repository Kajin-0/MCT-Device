## Evidence vocabulary

Use exactly:

- `RP` — direct Smith/RP-01 evidence.
- `SL` — same UWA/Faraone/Dell/Smith process-lineage primary evidence.
- `PT` — primary transfer evidence from an analogous process/apparatus or official technical source.
- `DER` — transparent derivation.
- `SYN` — explicit locally executable implementation synthesized from evidence/physics.
- `OPEN` — consequential coordinate for which no defensible numerical value is presently supported.

`OPEN` is a valid scientific state. A similar apparatus from another laboratory does not convert an RP-01 historical unknown to `RP`.

# Round-63 targeted LPE result

Round 62 identified Suh 1992 and Shinohara 1994 as the final paper-level targets most likely to close remaining LPE coordinates. Both full texts are now recovered.

## 1. Suh 1992 — independent x≈0.30 liquid branch

For nominal Hg0.7Cd0.3Te, Suh reports a Te-rich liquid approximately:

`Hg0.158 Cd0.012 Te0.830`

which is `xL≈0.0706, yL=.830` in the Round-61 notation, with a reported growth range around `500–489 °C`.

This is an independent `PT` neighborhood near the Honeywell `xL=.082/yL=.810/TL=507 °C -> xS≈.29` center. Do not average them.

Suh source preparation:

- elemental Hg/Cd/Te;
- vacuum-sealed quartz;
- `700 °C / 12 h`;
- water quench;
- ~2.5-g solution/run.

This independently strengthens the 700 °C source-synthesis family. It does not establish a historical RP-01 dwell or ampoule geometry.

## 2. Suh — Hg-vapor throttle geometry

Suh uses separate solution/HgTe wells under a tightly bolted graphite cover and a conical graphite button over the solution well.

Useful transport form:

`J_Hg,in ∝ D(P_HgTe-P_solution)d_hole²/t_button`.

External loss depends separately on slider/body gap.

For its nominal x≈0.30 branch, a central button hole near **2 mm** drove net solution-weight change close to zero in that apparatus.

Correct transfer rule:

- historical RP-01 button/throttle geometry: `OPEN`;
- Suh 2-mm x≈0.30 branch: `PT`;
- local objective: `Delta m_solution≈0` over the qualified growth cycle while meeting x/thickness/morphology, not literal reproduction of 2 mm.

## 3. Suh — thermal branch and empirical capability

Reported x≈0.30 sequence:

- homogenize ~20 °C above liquidus;
- cool, then ~0.3 °C/min;
- contact ~1 °C below liquidus;
- grow ~30 min while ramping;
- separate/cool rapidly.

Reported capability:

- mean `x≈0.304`;
- run-to-run `sigma_x≈0.003`;
- spatial variation ~`±0.002`;
- interface width ~`2–3 µm`;
- mirror-like/terraced surfaces.

These are PT benchmarks, not RP-01 acceptance criteria.

## 4. Shinohara — source mass is a trajectory

Reported branch:

- conventional graphite slider + HgTe reservoir;
- quartz tube 100 mm ID;
- H2 1 atm, 0.5 L/min;
- source 746 K; source melting point 727 K;
- growth start ~130 min;
- growth period 30 min;
- 2-g and 3-g HgTe reservoirs compared.

The experiment weighs source and reservoir and shows that the source may gain Hg before growth and lose it later. Therefore distinguish conceptually:

`m_solution,load`, `m_solution,growth-start`, `m_solution,post`, `m_Hg-source,load`, `m_Hg-source,post`.

When continuous weighing is impossible, establish the trajectory with sacrificial/dummy time-point cycles.

## 5. Shinohara — numerical wipe clearance

The highest-value recovered apparatus coordinate:

For x≈0.31 sample CZT4 on `(111) Cd0.97Zn0.03Te`, 10×10×1 mm:

- equilibrium cooling (`DeltaT=0`);
- `0.15 K/min`;
- complete wipe over 10×10 mm;
- slider-bottom/substrate-surface clearance ~**20 µm**;
- clearance variation `<~5 µm` identified as important;
- layer thickness ~4 µm;
- interdiffusion width ~2 µm;
- x≈0.31;
- slightly terraced/specular surface.

A second equilibrium branch used ~20–25 µm clearance.

Round-61 correction:

- historical RP-01/Fermionics clearance remains `OPEN`;
- a directly relevant numerical `20 µm PT` branch now exists;
- do not use 20 µm as a universal optimum or assume it transfers from a 4-µm layer to the RP-01 ~9.5-µm target without qualification.

## 6. Shinohara — wipe/thickness coupling

- equilibrium cooling: ~2–4-µm layers, best wiping;
- 15-K supercooling/step cooling: ~30–41-µm layers.

Therefore optimize `{DeltaT_contact, dT/dt, t_contact, clearance, wipe_geometry, Hg-transfer state}` jointly against `{thickness, x, residual melt, scratches, terraces}`.

## 7. Shinohara — growth-window Hg loss

The discussed branch loses ~`0.014 g` source liquid during growth despite Hg-vapor supply, approximately 0.7% on the ~2-g source scale.

This is not a release limit. It proves that sub-percent source-mass change is compositionally consequential and must be correlated with P06 x/cutoff before tuning temperature.
