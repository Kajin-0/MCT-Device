# P26B - Au/HgCdTe interface-chemistry transfer addendum

**Status:** `PT` INTERFACE EVIDENCE. Supplements P09/P09A/P26/P26A.

## Purpose

Davis et al. 1984 shows that depositing nominally unreactive Au onto HgCdTe can substantially alter the semiconductor surface chemistry and band bending. The result strengthens process-state requirements for RP-01 Cr/Au deposition without transferring that paper's p-type cleaved-surface behavior directly to the RIE-converted n+ contact region.

## Davis 1984 branch

Experimental state:

- UHV-cleaved p-Hg0.72Cd0.28Te(110);
- Au evaporated from a tungsten basket;
- thickness monitored by quartz oscillator;
- base/measurement vacuum of order 1e-9 Torr;
- UPS used to track chemical/electronic state versus Au coverage.

Observed behavior:

- strong Te redistribution/out-diffusion during Au deposition;
- large decrease in near-surface cation-to-anion ratio with increasing Au coverage;
- some Hg/Cd ratio change at thicker Au coverages;
- initial band-bending shift of order 0.2 eV toward flat-band/accumulation;
- evidence that metal deposition cannot be treated as adding an electrically inert film onto an unchanged semiconductor surface.

## Process consequence

For local Cr/Au transfer qualification retain/record:

- semiconductor surface-preparation ID;
- time from oxide clear/RIE to vacuum load;
- load-lock/base pressure;
- residual-gas or vacuum-quality record where available;
- Cr and Au source identities;
- deposition rates and time-resolved thickness;
- QCM crystal/geometry/tooling factor;
- source-to-sample distance/angle;
- sample holder and sample temperature or upper bound;
- breaks/delays between Cr and Au layers;
- post-deposition contact resistance and noise state.

## Evidence limit

This paper is not the RP-01 contact stack. It studies Au on cleaved p-type x=.28 material, whereas RP-01 uses Cr/Au on an RIE-converted n-type contact region. Therefore it supplies interface-chemistry rationale and metrology requirements, not RP numerical deposition setpoints.
