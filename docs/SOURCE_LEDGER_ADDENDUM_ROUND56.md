# Source-ledger addendum — Round 56 adversarial repair

**Date:** 2026-08-16 America/New_York

## Governing rule reinforced

Round 56 makes a stricter distinction between:

`literature ancestry of each scalar value`

and

`empirical validity of the combined process state`.

A set of individually sourced numbers does not become an empirically established process merely because every number has a citation.

Evidence codes remain:

- `RP` — direct Smith/RP-01 evidence;
- `SL` — same-lineage evidence;
- `PT` — primary transfer evidence;
- `DER` — derived quantity;
- `SYN` — synthesized empirical starting implementation.

## Critical value-level provenance added in Round 56

### Smith et al. 2001

Publication locations are now carried into the protocol for critical downstream anchors:

- starting Fermionics material specification: experimental section, around p. 458;
- Mask-2 `4–5 µm`, `80 °C / 30 min`, chlorobenzene `30 min`, Cr `300 Å`, Au `2700 Å`: device-fabrication section, p. 458;

- detector measurement context `80 K / 10 V cm^-1 / stated 60° FOV / 4 µm`: Fig. 3, p. 459.

Transfer rule: RP-01 is the strongest downstream fabrication/measurement anchor. It does not supply the upstream LPE growth traveler because the material was purchased.

### Honeywell US4317689A

Transfer delta: composition-matched tie line and covered/Hg-source slider concept; absolute charge mass, exact boat geometry and Round-56 thermal/contact schedule are not supplied by the tie-line table.

### Harman low-temperature Hg-rich branch

`~250 °C / 1 h / Hg-saturated` remains a kinetic/process-family anchor.

### Honeywell US4081819A cryogenic attachment

Controlled comparison retained:

- glass-adhesive branch cracked;
- silicone branches at both 40-g and 5-g thermocompression conditions survived.

Round-56 interpretation: compliant silicone attachment is supported as the causal mechanical family. The evidence does not establish 5 g as an optimum.

## Numerical precision rule strengthened

Internal calculations may retain sufficient digits for reproducibility, but the displayed process value should communicate:

1. nominal setpoint;
2. instrument resolution/realizable increment;
3. calibration uncertainty;
4. research-screening tolerance or acceptance rule where justified.

Examples:

- source masses are displayed at the calibrated 0.1-mg balance scale rather than carrying arbitrary extra digits;
- interpreted RIE individual flows are displayed as `10.7` and `53.3 sccm`, while the direct RP authority remains total `64 sccm` and printed `CH4/5H2`;
- Cr/Au rates are given with explicit SYN rate bands rather than as infinitely precise setpoints.

## Permanent restrictions

- `SYN` does not become `RP`, `SL`, or `PT` through repetition.
- a transfer value does not prove transferability of its neighboring process values.
- a DOE center is not an optimum until supported by response data.
- a non-starved Hg reservoir is not proof of defect equilibrium.
- a good TLM result is not proof of minority-carrier blocking.
- a detector cutoff is not a material band-gap wavelength.
- a package time constant is not automatically a carrier lifetime.
