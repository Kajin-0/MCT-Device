## S36-01 — Srivastav et al. 2005 mesa paper

**Reference**  
V. Srivastav, R. Pal, B. L. Sharma, A. Naik, D. S. Rawal, V. Gopal, H. P. Vyas, “Etching of Mesa Structures in HgCdTe,” *Journal of Electronic Materials* 34 (2005) 1440–1445. DOI `10.1007/s11664-005-0203-5`.

**Class:** `PRIMARY-X028-MESA`.

**Directly recovered**

- x = 0.28 HgCdTe;
- Br2 varied nominally 1–3%;
- selected `2% Br2`;
- selected `3:1 EG:HBr`;
- also represented as `75% EG / 25% HBr`;
- reference data at 21 °C;
- mean process-variation vertical rate about 2.78 µm/min;
- mean anisotropy about 0.63;
- lower-temperature morphology example at 10 °C;
- Br2 volatility identified as a source of reproducibility problems.

**Not recovered from accessible full text**

- Br2 basis (`w/w`, `v/v`, `w/v`, molarity, other);
- EG:HBr mass/volume basis;
- HBr stock assay;
- mixing order;
- bath volume;
- actual agitation method/rate;
- exact patterned-mesa quench/rinse/dry sequence.

**Disposition:** source notation is direct; executable preparation remains open.

## S36-02 — Srivastav/Pal/Vyas 2005 review

**Reference**  
V. Srivastav, R. Pal, H. P. Vyas, “Overview of etching technologies used for HgCdTe,” *Opto-Electronics Review* 13(3) (2005) 197–211.

**Class:** `SAME-AUTHOR-REVIEW / CONVENTION-EVIDENCE`.

**Recovered**

Wet-etch summary includes:

- Br2/methanol `1–10%` with an etch-rate relation explicitly referenced to `[Br] in vol%`;
- Br2/HBr `1–5%`;
- Br2/HBr/ethylene glycol “in various ratios.”

**Interpretation**

This shows contemporaneous authors explicitly used volume-percent notation for at least one liquid-bromine HgCdTe etchant family.

**Restriction**

The Br2/HBr/EG row does not explicitly state that its Br2 percentage is volume percent. It therefore supports, but does not prove, a v/v interpretation of the mesa formulation.

## S36-03 — same-SSPL overlapping-author passivation patent

**Reference**  
“A Process For Passivation Of Mercury Cadmium Telluride,” Director General, DRDO, India; filing date 10 Nov 2003; publication 45/2005; inventors include Ravinder Pal and Vishnu Gopal, Solid State Physics Laboratory, Delhi.

**Class:** `SAME-SSPL-CONVENTION / PRIMARY-RINSE-HANDOFF-TRANSFER`.

**Directly recovered**

The claims explicitly define bromine/methanol concentration as `v/v`:

- `0.1–1%`, preferably `0.4–0.6% (v/v)` Br2/MeOH for chemo-mechanical polish/free etch;
- working example `0.5% (v/v)`;
- second free-etch stage `0.01–0.1%`, preferably `0.03–0.07% (v/v)`;
- working example `0.05% (v/v)`.

The same process directly specifies methanol rinsing, running DI-water rinsing, and transfer into electrochemical cleaning without exposure to air.

**Interpretation**

This is the strongest recovered evidence that the same SSPL/overlapping-author laboratory used explicit v/v bromine notation in the same period.

**Restriction**

Chemistry is Br2/methanol, not Br2/HBr/EG. Therefore:

`SAME-SSPL v/v convention != direct Srivastav mesa basis`.

## S36-04 — Leech/Gwynn/Kibel 1989 counterexample

**Reference**  
P. W. Leech, P. J. Gwynn, M. H. Kibel, “A selective etchant for Hg1−xCdxTe, CdTe and HgTe on GaAs,” *Applied Surface Science* 37 (1989) 291–298. DOI `10.1016/0169-4332(89)90491-1`.

**Class:** `PRIMARY-CONVENTION-COUNTEREXAMPLE`.

**Directly recovered**

Comparison HgCdTe etchant explicitly written:

`0.1% (w/w) Br:HBr`.

**Importance**

Weight/weight convention demonstrably existed in HgCdTe bromine/HBr literature. No universal conversion from “% bromine” to v/v is permitted.

## S36-05 — US4436580A

**Reference**  
US4436580A, “Method of preparing a mercury cadmium telluride substrate for passivation and processing.”

**Class:** `PRIMARY-CONVENTION-TRANSFER / PRIMARY-RINSE-HANDOFF-TRANSFER`.

**Directly recovered**

- dip etch using `2% bromine / 98% solution (V/V)` in bromine/methanol branch;
- approximately 20–45 s;
- methanol quench until bromine traces removed;
- acetone/methanol rinse;
- immediate dry nitrogen;
- later polishing solution described using EG plus a bromine/methanol mixture.

**Restriction:** different process family; no direct transfer of formula/time to RP-01 mesa.

# Round-36 evidence conclusion

The evidence hierarchy is now:

1. `2% Br2 / 3:1 EG:HBr` — direct Srivastav notation and output data;
2. `v/v` — strongest current **same-laboratory candidate convention**;
3. `w/w` — demonstrably used in other primary HgCdTe Br:HBr literature;
4. exact Srivastav basis — still open;
5. exact RP-01/UWA wet-mesa formula — still open.

P28A therefore creates `CANDIDATE-VV-SAME-LAB` but prohibits relabeling it as direct historical fact.
