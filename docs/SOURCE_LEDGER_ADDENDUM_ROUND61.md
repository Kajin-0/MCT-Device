# Source-ledger addendum — Round 61 consequential-coordinate audit

**Date:** 2026-08-17 America/New_York

## Evidence-state rule

Round 61 formally adds `OPEN` to the publication-layer evidence vocabulary.

- `RP`: direct reference-process evidence.
- `SL`: same-lineage primary evidence.
- `PT`: primary transfer or official technical evidence.
- `DER`: transparent derivation.
- `SYN`: explicit local empirical implementation.
- `OPEN`: consequential coordinate remains unresolved; no defensible numerical value presently supported.

Only documentary recovery can establish historical `RP`/`SL` identity. Local experiments establish local `SYN` behavior, not historical fact.

## Mercury thermodynamic warning

NIST mercury data establish that 700 °C lies well above the normal boiling point and outside the cited lower-temperature Antoine-fit range. This is used as a **restriction**, not as an ampoule-pressure derivation.

`P_ampoule(700 °C) != P_Hg,pure(700 °C)` for the Hg-Cd-Te synthesis charge.

Actual ternary-source pressure and minimum safe ampoule geometry remain OPEN pending appropriate thermodynamics and vessel engineering.
