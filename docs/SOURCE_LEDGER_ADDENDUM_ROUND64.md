## Evidence rule

The papers are classified by relationship to RP-01, not by prestige. `SL` is reserved for the UWA/Faraone/Dell/Smith process lineage; Honeywell and other high-value process papers remain `PT` unless they are direct RP-01 evidence. Review material is retained as context and does not independently promote hard setpoints.

## R64-S01 - Schmit, Hager and Wood 1982 - PT (Honeywell upstream lineage)

J.L. Schmit, R.J. Hager, R.A. Wood, “Liquid phase epitaxy of Hg1-xCdxTe,” *Journal of Crystal Growth* 56 (1982) 485-489, DOI `10.1016/0022-0248(82)90468-7`.

Recovered: high-purity graphite horizontal slider; horizontal quartz tube; flowing high-purity atmospheric H2; external regulated furnace; push rod; (111) CdTe 1-mm substrates up to 2 x 3 cm; heat above liquidus then supercool; isothermal growth; typical 1-20-min growth for <=~30-um layers; thickness to ~50 um; strong run-to-run composition control.

Disposition: strengthens P03/P30 Honeywell-family thermal/time/composition control; historical RP-01 machine dimensions remain OPEN.

## R64-S02 - Nemirovsky et al. 1982 - PT

Y. Nemirovsky, S. Margalit, E. Finkman, Y. Shacham-Diamand, I. Kidron, “Growth and properties of Hg1-xCdxTe epitaxial layers,” *Journal of Electronic Materials* 11 (1982) 133-153.

Recovered: semiclosed x=0.2-0.3 slider; ~4.5-g HgTe reservoir; ~3.5-g solution; vapor-communication groove; reservoir renewed each run; solution reused for several runs; ~20% reservoir mass loss over a typical 2-3 h branch near 460 C; explicit ~463 C equilibration / 5 C supercool / 458->455 C / 0.25 C min^-1 / ~10 um in 15 min example; pre-load polishing/Br2-methanol branch; substrate vapor exposure during equilibration.

Disposition: PT process-state strengthening only.

## R64-S03 - Smith et al. 1999 - SL

E.P.G. Smith, C.A. Musca, D.A. Redfern, J.M. Dell, L. Faraone, “Reactive ion etching for mesa structuring in HgCdTe,” *Journal of Vacuum Science & Technology A* 17 (1999) 2503, DOI `10.1116/1.581988`.

Recovered: Plasma Technology parallel plate; 16 C cathode; CH4/5H2 notation; 30 sccm total; 400 mTorr; 0.4 W/cm2; printed 180-V dc bias; ~0.15 um/min x=.31 etch; 1.047-um LBIC implementation; 200 C / 17 h sealed-Hg recovery branch.

Disposition: P34 same-lineage physical-state evidence strengthened; exact RP-01 self-bias/electrode state OPEN.

## R64-S04 - Smith et al. 2000 - SL

E.P.G. Smith, C.A. Musca, D.A. Redfern, J.M. Dell, L. Faraone, “H2-Based Dry Plasma Etching for Mesa Structuring of HgCdTe,” *Journal of Electronic Materials* 29 (2000) 853-858, DOI `10.1007/s11664-000-0237-7`.

Recovered: n-type Fermionics LPE x=.31 PC detector comparison; literal printed `H2/5CH4` mixture; 30 sccm; 400 mTorr; 0.4 W/cm2; 16 C cathode; 80-V dc bias; 70 min; ~0.15 um/min; ~10-um lateral n+ extension; direct responsivity/lifetime/noise/D* degradation relative to wet mesa processing.

Disposition: direct SL detector-performance penalty for large-area/long-dose RIE; gas notation preserved literally.

## R64-S05 - Smith et al. 1998 - SL

E.P.G. Smith, J.F. Siliquini, C.A. Musca, J. Antoszewski, J.M. Dell, L. Faraone, J. Piotrowski, “Mercury annealing of reactive ion etching induced p- to n-type conversion in extrinsically doped p-type HgCdTe,” *Journal of Applied Physics* 83 (1998) 5555, DOI `10.1063/1.367389`.

Recovered: x=.31; 400 mTorr CH4/H2, 90 W RIE branch; sealed-Hg 200 C / 17 h anneal removed LBIC-visible converted region and restored p-type Hall state.

Disposition: RIE/Hg-anneal reversibility SL branch; not an RP-01 repair recipe.

## R64-S06 - White et al. 2001 - SL

J. White, R. Pal, J.M. Dell, C.A. Musca, J. Antoszewski, L. Faraone, P. Burke, “p-to-n Type-Conversion Mechanisms for HgCdTe Exposed to H2/CH4 Plasmas,” *Journal of Electronic Materials* 30 (2001) 762-767, DOI `10.1007/BF02665869`.

Recovered: D2/CH4 SIMS tracer branch; D2 54 sccm / CH4 18 sccm; 100 mTorr; 0.2 W/cm2; ~200-V dc bias; 15 C stage; 80/100 C post-bake redistribution; multi-population hydrogen mechanism.

Disposition: mechanistic SL evidence; stage temperature explicitly distinguished from actual sample temperature.

## R64-S07 - Nguyen et al. 2002 - SL

T. Nguyen, J. Antoszewski, C.A. Musca, D.A. Redfern, J.M. Dell, L. Faraone, “Transport Properties of Reactive-Ion-Etching-Induced p-to-n Type Converted Layers in HgCdTe,” *Journal of Electronic Materials* 31 (2002), DOI `10.1007/s11664-002-0214-4`.

Recovered: x=.31 and .23 Fermionics LPE material; CH4/H2 1:3; 0.4 W/cm2; 2 min; ~0.3-um physical recession; differential Hall/QMSA; thin damaged surface-electron layer plus deeper high-mobility converted bulk region.

Disposition: P05/P34 multicarrier interpretation strengthened.

## R64-S08 - Antoszewski et al. 2000 - SL

J. Antoszewski, C.A. Musca, J.M. Dell, L. Faraone, “Characterization of Hg0.7Cd0.3Te n- on p-type structures obtained by reactive ion etching induced p- to n conversion,” *Journal of Electronic Materials* 29 (2000), DOI `10.1007/s11664-000-0234-x`.

Recovered: x=.30 LPE; ~100 W; ~40 sccm; 5:1 H2/CH4; ~500 mTorr; 2 min; converted depth 2.0 +/- 0.5 um; 30-400 K/0-12 T Hall/QMSA; high-mobility bulk converted electrons plus low-mobility surface electrons.

Disposition: SL multicarrier/depth evidence.

## R64-S09 - Musca et al. 1998 - SL

C.A. Musca, J.F. Siliquini, E.P.G. Smith, J.M. Dell, L. Faraone, “Laser Beam Induced Current Imaging of Reactive Ion Etching Induced n-Type Doping in HgCdTe,” *Journal of Electronic Materials* 27 (1998), DOI `10.1007/s11664-998-0032-4`.

Recovered: x=.31 Fermionics n-type; H2 27 sccm / CH4 5 sccm; 430 mTorr; 0.4 W/cm2; 18 C cathode; ~200-V dc bias; 30 s; ~0.075-um physical recession; electrical n+/n signature to ~8 um after depth calibration; strong mask-resist edge-profile effect on apparent lateral conversion.

Disposition: approximately 8-um SL branch largely closed; RP-01 conversion depth remains OPEN. Resist thickness/profile promoted to first-class RIE coordinate.

## R64-S10 - Musca et al. 1999 - SL measurement lineage

C.A. Musca, D.A. Redfern, E.P.G. Smith, J.M. Dell, L. Faraone, J. Bajaj, “Junction Depth Measurement in HgCdTe Using Laser Beam Induced Current (LBIC),” *Journal of Electronic Materials* 28 (1999), DOI `10.1007/s11664-999-0042-x`.

Recovered: calibrated 2/4/6-um junction structures; 1.047-um CW, ~3-um spot, ~400 mW/cm2, 80 K; demonstrated LBIC depth sensitivity to doping, wavelength, illumination direction, geometry and diffusion length.

Disposition: P37 absolute-depth claims require calibrated/model-supported interpretation.

## R64-S11 - Pal et al. 1999 - PT

R. Pal, B.L. Sharma, V. Gopal, V. Kumar, O.P. Agnihotri, “Effect of HgCdTe-passivant interface properties on the responsivity performance of photoconductive detectors,” *Infrared Physics & Technology* 40 (1999) 101-107, DOI `10.1016/S1350-4495(98)00052-8`.

Recovered: gated PC experiment coupling surface accumulation, shunt resistance, effective lifetime and responsivity; 300-A anodic oxide + 2700-A ZnS + 300-A In gate example; fitted Dit of order 5-7e11 cm^-2 eV^-1.

Disposition: passivation acceptance vector strengthened; values PT only.

## R64-S12 - Bhan et al. 2004 - PT

R.K. Bhan, V. Gopal, R.S. Saxena, J.P. Singh, “Noise modeling of shunt resistance in HgCdTe photoconductor detectors,” *Infrared Physics & Technology* 45 (2004) 81-92, DOI `10.1016/j.infrared.2003.06.001`.

Recovered: surface-shunt resistance from anodic-oxide accumulation layers as an additional detector-noise path; fitted surface-potential/Qss example; coupled resistance/responsivity/lifetime/noise behavior.

Disposition: P12 surface-shunt noise branch added; no RP-01 numerical transfer.

## R64-S13 - Schoolar et al. 1982 - PT

R.B. Schoolar, B.K. Janousek, R.L. Alt, R.C. Carscallen, M.J. Daughtery, A.A. Fote, “Optical and field effect studies of the Hg0.7Cd0.3Te-anodic oxide interface,” *Journal of Vacuum Science & Technology* 21 (1982) 164, DOI `10.1116/1.571704`.

Recovered: x=.30 anodic-oxide positive charge ~5e11 cm^-2; visible-light threshold ~2.2 eV for charge neutralization; dark recovery from minutes at room temperature to weeks at 77 K; electric-field-driven charge exchange; optical oxide gap ~3.4 eV.

Disposition: passivation electrical-state memory/preconditioning becomes explicit.

## R64-S14 - Davis et al. 1984 - PT

G.D. Davis, W.A. Beck, N.E. Byer, R.R. Daniels, G. Margaritondo, “Deposition of Au overlayers onto cleaved (Hg,Cd)Te surfaces,” *Journal of Vacuum Science & Technology A* 2 (1984) 546, DOI `10.1116/1.572442`.

Recovered: UHV-cleaved p-Hg0.72Cd0.28Te(110); tungsten-basket Au evaporation; quartz-oscillator thickness; ~1e-9 Torr; strong Te redistribution and cation/anion change; ~0.2-eV initial band-bending shift.

Disposition: P26 interface-chemistry state strengthened; not equivalent to RP-01 Cr/Au on RIE-converted n+ HgCdTe.

## R64-S15 - Nemirovsky and Bahir 1989 - Secondary-A

Y. Nemirovsky, G. Bahir, “Passivation of mercury cadmium telluride surfaces,” *Journal of Vacuum Science & Technology A* 7 (1989) 450, DOI `10.1116/1.576202`.

Recovered/corroborated: passivation technology framework; practical anodic oxide family; typical fixed-charge/interface-state ranges; thermal and chemical limitations.

Disposition: cross-check and literature map only; primary sources control hard process statements.
