# Source ledger addendum — Round 35: LPE absolute apparatus / charge / contact trajectory

**Date:** 2026-08-16 America/New_York

## Scope

Round 35 re-audited the highest-priority P16A LPE execution blocker: whether primary literature closes the absolute growth-charge mass, boat dimensions, source inventory and growth trajectory for the x≈0.30 RP-01/Fermionics material branch.

The main result is a firm evidence boundary:

**Honeywell primary sources close the apparatus topology and composition tie line much more strongly than the absolute hardware scale. They do not recover an executable dimensioned boat or x≈0.29 charge mass.**

---

## S35-01 — Bowers & Schmit / Honeywell US4317689A

**Class:** `DIRECT-HONEYWELL-PATENT`  
**Use:** primary composition + apparatus architecture.

Recovered direct facts include:

- covered graphite horizontal-slider/stator architecture;
- substrate recess in the base;
- movable slider with growth-solution through-well(s);
- graphite plug over the growth-solution well;
- separate shallow Hg-source recess;
- Hg-distribution grooves/moats;
- close-fitting graphite cover;
- quartz-tube furnace;
- nitrogen purge followed by flowing hydrogen;
- charge heated above liquidus and growth performed below liquidus;
- composition table including `xL=.082`, `yL=.810`, `TL=507 °C`, `xS≈.29`.

The more preferred embodiment additionally describes:

- two through-wells;
- through-well taper with smaller bottom opening;
- top and bottom Hg moats/grooves;
- recessed areas under/around well positions generally corresponding in size to the substrate recess.

### Round-35 negative extraction

Targeted inspection of the recovered patent text did **not** recover:

- numerical well depth;
- numerical well diameter/width;
- total well volume;
- absolute charge mass;
- numerical substrate-recess dimensions;
- numerical cover/slider clearance;
- N2/H2 flow rates;
- exact thermocouple location;
- exact x≈.29 growth time/thickness pair.

Searches for units/terms such as `inch`, `cm`, `dimension`, and `gram` did not produce a usable apparatus-scale value for this branch.

**Disposition:** strong geometry/topology source; insufficient for machining or weighing an exact historical x≈.29 recipe.

## S35-04 — Radhakrishnan, Sitharaman & Gupta 2003

J. K. Radhakrishnan, S. Sitharaman, S. C. Gupta, *Journal of Crystal Growth* 252 (2003) 79–86, DOI `10.1016/S0022-0248(02)02530-7`.

**Class:** `PRIMARY-TE-RICH-SLIDER-TRANSFER`.

Direct quantitative apparatus/process evidence:

- high-purity/high-density graphite slider system;
- CdZnTe substrate approximately `15 × 15 × 1 mm`;
- solution-bin block and separate HgTe cavity under tight graphite cover;
- horizontal quartz tube with gas, push-pull slider and thermocouple provisions;
- `10 g` synthesized Te-rich growth compound in representative source-preparation branch;
- 6N elements;
- evacuated-quartz synthesis at `700 °C / 8 h`;
- synthesized material ground/mixed;
- approximately `4.8 g` growth charge per run;
- `3 g` HgTe reservoir per run;
- smooth/uniform slide-out emphasized to reduce voids, pinholes and melt trapping.

**Restriction:** different composition/apparatus branch. The `4.8 g` and `3 g` values are not Honeywell x=.29 values and may not be scaled by substrate area.

## S35-05 — Harman 1980

T. C. Harman, *Journal of Electronic Materials* 9 (1980), DOI `10.1007/BF02822728`, “Liquidus isotherms, solidus lines and LPE growth in the Te-rich corner of the Hg-Cd-Te system.”

**Class:** `PRIMARY-HARMAN-1980`.

Retained direct transfer evidence:

- Te-rich HgCdTe LPE over roughly `450–550 °C` represented conditions;
- growth times roughly `0.25–10 min` in the reported experimental branch;
- approximately `1 h at 550 °C` solution equilibration in the represented branch;
- reported layers approximately `3–15 µm` in the high-quality examples discussed in project records;
- horizontal slider / flowing-H2 process context.

**Restriction:** this is not the Honeywell xL=.082/yL=.810 apparatus traveler.

## S35-07 — Harman / MIT US4642142A

**Class:** `PRIMARY-HARMAN-MIT-PATENT / TRANSFER-TRAJECTORY`.

Useful direct quantitative trajectory data include represented branches with:

- supercooling around `2–10 °C`;
- solution/source equilibration about `0.5–2 h`, typically around 60 min in the described approach;
- substrate contact from seconds to about 30 min depending on branch;
- Table examples including `4 × 10 mm` and `8 × 8 mm` substrates;
- a `4 × 10 mm` example producing about `7 µm` after about 1 min under its specific trajectory;
- `8 × 8 mm` examples producing about `30–33 µm` after about 30 min under another specific slow-cooling trajectory;
- explicit dependence on cooling rate and supercooling.

Targeted inspection did not recover a universal growth-solution gram loading.

**Use:** demonstrates quantitatively that thickness is trajectory-dependent and that contact time cannot be selected without the local thermal branch.

## S35-10 — Fermionics/UWA starting-material lineage

Same-era UWA primary work identifies Fermionics-supplied LPE HgCdTe/CdZnTe starting material, including:

- an n-type photoconductor material branch around `x=.31`, `N_D≈3.4×10^14 cm^-3` in UWA two-dimensional modeling work;
- a separate p-type MWIR LPE branch about `24 µm` thick with a roughly 4.3-µm cutoff at 80 K in UWA photodiode work.

**Class:** `SAME-UWA / FERMIONICS-MATERIAL-LINEAGE`.

**Use:** establishes that Fermionics supplied relevant LPE material to UWA.

**Negative result:** no recovered Fermionics process traveler provided internal boat dimensions, absolute charge mass, Hg-source mass, or exact trajectory.

Therefore:

`FERMIONICS MATERIAL PROVENANCE != FERMIONICS PROCESS RECIPE`.

## S35-11 — Radhakrishnan-area scaling rejected

A tempting inference would be to take the `~4.8 g` / `15 × 15 mm` Radhakrishnan branch and rescale it to another substrate area.

**Disposition:** rejected.

Reason:

- well depth/shape unknown relative to target boat;
- hot fill fraction unknown;
- free-surface/Hg-loss geometry differs;
- overlap and source depletion differ;
- solution composition differs;
- thermal field differs.

This failed inference is preserved so it is not rediscovered later.
