# Source ledger addendum — source-recovery round 6

**Date:** 2026-08-15 America/New_York

This addendum records the RIE/LBIC/reactor/transport sources promoted or materially reinterpreted after round 5.

## S-R6-01 — Siliquini et al. 1997 — direct depth-versus-physical-etch demonstration

**Class:** Primary-A / Same-UWA-lineage  
**Citation:** J. F. Siliquini, J. M. Dell, C. A. Musca, L. Faraone, “Scanning Laser Microscopy of Reactive Ion Etching Induced n-Type Conversion in Vacancy-Doped p-Type HgCdTe,” *Applied Physics Letters* 70, 3443–3445 (1997).  
**DOI:** `10.1063/1.119159`

**Direct institutional-record anchors:**

- vacancy-doped p-type Hg0.69Cd0.31Te;
- CH4/H2 RIE;
- pressure `410 mTorr`;
- RF power density `0.4 W/cm²`;
- physical material removal `~0.2 µm`;
- electrical n-type conversion depth `~1.5 µm`.

**Role:** Direct proof that electrical conversion depth can greatly exceed physical etch depth.

**Restriction:** p-type branch, not the RP-01 n-type blocking-contact process.

## S-R6-02 — Siliquini et al. 1998 — arsenic-doped LBIC/donor-density branch

**Class:** Primary-A / Same-UWA-lineage  
**Citation:** J. F. Siliquini, J. M. Dell, C. A. Musca, E. P. G. Smith, L. Faraone, J. Piotrowski, “Estimation of doping density in HgCdTe p-n junctions using scanning laser microscopy,” *Applied Physics Letters* 72 (1998).  
**DOI:** `10.1063/1.120642`

**UWA institutional-record anchors:**

- arsenic-doped p-type Hg0.71Cd0.29Te;
- prior Hg anneal to eliminate Hg vacancies;
- CH4/H2 RIE;
- pressure `340 mTorr`;
- RF power density `0.4 W/cm²`;
- LBIC over 80–300 K;
- SEMICAD DEVICE modeling used to extract effective donor density.

**Source discrepancy:** several secondary/reposted records print `390 mTorr`. For controlled use, `340 mTorr` is preferred because it comes from the UWA institutional primary-paper record. Preserve the 390-mTorr value only as a secondary transcription discrepancy until publisher full text is acquired.

## S-R6-03 — Siliquini et al. 1998 JCG — lateral/vertical conversion characterization

**Class:** Primary-A / Same-UWA-lineage  
**Citation:** J. F. Siliquini, J. M. Dell, C. A. Musca, L. Faraone, J. Piotrowski, “Characterisation of reactive-ion-etching-induced type-conversion in p-type HgCdTe using scanning laser microscopy,” *Journal of Crystal Growth* 184–185, 1219–1222 (1998).  
**DOI:** `10.1016/S0022-0248(98)80255-8`

**Role:** Primary method lineage for lateral/vertical conversion extraction in x≈0.3 HgCdTe using LBIC and modeling.

## S-R6-04 — Musca et al. 1999 — LBIC junction-depth method

**Class:** Primary-A / Same-UWA-lineage  
**Citation:** C. A. Musca, D. A. Redfern, E. P. G. Smith, J. M. Dell, L. Faraone, J. Bajaj, “Junction Depth Measurement in HgCdTe Using Laser Beam Induced Current (LBIC),” *Journal of Electronic Materials* 28(6), 603–610 (1999).  
**DOI:** `10.1007/s11664-999-0042-x`

**Role:** Non-destructive junction-depth method development. The paper explicitly studies sensitivity to junction doping density, illumination wavelength, front/back illumination and test-structure geometry.

**Use:** P08 depth extraction must retain these model/input dependencies; a single LBIC contrast number is not a geometry-independent depth measurement.

## S-R6-05 — Smith et al. 1999 — mesa RIE power-density branch

**Class:** Primary-A / Same-UWA-lineage  
**Citation:** E. P. G. Smith, C. A. Musca, D. A. Redfern, J. M. Dell, L. Faraone, “Reactive ion etching for mesa structuring in HgCdTe,” *Journal of Vacuum Science & Technology A* 17(5), 2503–2509 (1999).  
**DOI:** `10.1116/1.581988`

**Direct abstract-level process anchors:**

- pressure `400 mTorr`;
- printed gas notation `CH4/5H2`;
- RF power density `0.4 W/cm²`;
- p→n conversion in p-type material and n+ modification in n-type material;
- ~200 °C Hg anneal restores initial electrical properties in the studied mesa-RIE branch.

**Important unresolved point:** accessible text does not prove this study used the identical powered-electrode geometry/configuration as RP-01.

## S-R6-06 — Smith et al. 2000 — in-situ vacuum processing bridge

**Class:** Primary conference / Same-UWA-lineage  
**Citation:** E. P. G. Smith, K. J. Winchester, C. A. Musca, J. M. Dell, L. Faraone, “Dry plasma technology for in-situ vacuum processing of HgCdTe infrared photodetectors,” Proceedings of SIMC-XI, 2000, pp. 318–321.

**Role:** Highest-value archival bridge between RIE and metal deposition / in-vacuum HgCdTe processing.

**Current limitation:** UWA public record is metadata only. No reactor model/electrode geometry/RF frequency/base pressure/deposition sequence was recovered.

## S-R6-07 — Nguyen et al. 2002 — RIE multicarrier transport

**Class:** Primary-A / Same-UWA-lineage  
**Citation:** T. Nguyen, J. Antoszewski, C. A. Musca, D. A. Redfern, J. M. Dell, L. Faraone, “Transport Properties of Reactive-Ion-Etching-Induced p-to-n Type Converted Layers in HgCdTe,” *Journal of Electronic Materials* 31(7), 652–659 (2002).  
**DOI:** `10.1007/s11664-002-0214-4`

**Direct UWA-record conclusions:**

- x=0.23 and x=0.31 LPE HgCdTe;
- vacancy- and gold-doped p-type starting material;
- magnetic-field-dependent differential Hall/resistivity;
- QMSA resolves a damaged moderate-mobility surface electron layer and a deeper high-mobility n-type region;
- deeper electron transport is consistent with high-quality HgCdTe;
- dopant distribution is diffusion-like;
- conversion mechanism involves acceptor neutralization plus diffusion of extrinsic n-type dopants from the surface.

**Role:** Strong reason to require multicarrier analysis rather than automatically fit a uniform n+ slab.

## S-R6-08 — Antoszewski et al. 2000 — composition-matched multicarrier example

**Class:** Primary-A / Same-UWA-lineage  
**Citation:** J. Antoszewski, C. A. Musca, J. M. Dell, L. Faraone, “Characterization of Hg0.7Cd0.3Te n-on-p-type structures obtained by reactive ion etching induced p-to-n conversion,” *Journal of Electronic Materials* 29(6), 837–840 (2000).  
**DOI:** `10.1007/s11664-000-0234-x`

**Reported 77-K transport examples in a distinct p-type branch:**

- vacancy-doped deeper electrons: n≈3×10^15 cm^-3, µ≈4×10^4 cm²/Vs;
- Au-doped deeper electrons: n≈1.5×10^15 cm^-3, µ≈6×10^4 cm²/Vs;
- surface electron sheets: approximately 9×10^12 to 1×10^13 cm^-2;
- surface-electron mobility roughly 1.5×10^3 to 1.5×10^4 cm²/Vs;
- converted layer described as ~2 µm thick.

**Restriction:** not RP-01 setpoints. Used only to demonstrate physically distinct surface and deeper electron channels near x=0.30.

## Round-6 rejected inference — 125 cm² powered-electrode area

If RP-01 `50 W` and the 1999 same-UWA `0.4 W/cm²` referred to the same powered electrode and power-density definition, then a purely algebraic area would be:

`A = 50 / 0.4 = 125 cm²`, equivalent circular diameter ~12.6 cm.

This inference is **not accepted** because identical reactor/electrode geometry has not been proven. Do not use 125 cm² as historical tool geometry.
