# Source-ledger addendum — Round 62 full-text acquisition

**Date:** 2026-08-17 America/New_York  
**Use with:** `docs/RP01_FULLTEXT_SOURCE_ACQUISITION_ROUND62.md`

## Evidence rule

The supplied full papers are classified by relationship to RP-01, not merely by source quality. `RP` is direct RP-01, `SL` is same UWA/process lineage, `PT` is primary transfer evidence, `DER` is derivation, `SYN` is local implementation, and `OPEN` remains unresolved. A highly similar apparatus from another laboratory remains `PT`.

## Reviewed full-text sources

### R62-S01 — Chiang et al. 1988 — `PT`
C.D. Chiang, T.B. Wu, W.C. Chung, S.J. Yang, Y.M. Pang, “A new attachment for stable control of mercury pressure in the slider LPE of Hg1-xCdxTe,” *J. Crystal Growth* 87 (1988) 161–168.

Recovered: quartz two-arm cover; independently heated liquid-Hg reservoirs; ~4-g solution; 500 °C/50-min homogenization; 475 °C/30-min saturation; ~470→460 °C/30-min growth; ~0.15 °C/min; <0.5-g Hg consumption/cycle; apparatus/composition-specific reservoir-temperature sensitivity; ±0.002 run-to-run x example.

Disposition: P30/P30A Hg-source state strengthened; historical geometry remains `OPEN`.

### R62-S02 — Beck, Davis and Goldberg 1990 — `PT`
“Resistance and 1/f noise of Au, Al, and Ge contacts to (Hg,Cd)Te,” *J. Appl. Phys.* 67, 6340 (1990), DOI `10.1063/1.345154`.

Recovered: cryogenic contact resistance/noise; 10/30/100/300-µm circular pads; metal-dependent 1/f size scaling; ~1 Å/s initial deposition branch.

Disposition: P09/P12 contact-noise interpretation strengthened; not the RP-01 Cr/Au interface.

### R62-S03 — Chang et al. 2005 — `PT`
“Composition and thickness distribution of HgCdTe molecular beam epitaxy wafers by infrared microscope mapping,” *J. Crystal Growth* 277, 78–84 (2005), DOI `10.1016/j.jcrysgro.2005.01.051`.

Recovered: automated infrared-microscope mapping; adjustable aperture; LN2 HgCdTe detector/KBr/Ever-Glo implementation; interference-matrix model; Fourier-domain thickness initialization; Levenberg-Marquardt + simulated annealing; self-consistent x,d fitting.

Disposition: P06 mapping/model lineage strengthened; MBE model not substituted for LPE/Hougen model.

### R62-S04 — Gopal, Ashokan and Dhar 1992 — `PT`
“Compositional characterization of HgCdTe epilayers by infrared transmission,” *Infrared Physics* 33 (1992) 39–45.

Recovered: x=0.2–0.3 Hougen-based transmission analysis; explicit thickness dependence of the 50%-transmission edge; dual edge descriptors as a composition-uniformity diagnostic.

Disposition: P06 rationale strengthened.

### R62-S05 — Ngoc and Nha 1998 — `PT`
“The interface characteristics of passivity anodic oxide films on Hg0.8Cd0.2Te by C-V measurements,” *Thin Solid Films* 334 (1998) 40–43.

Recovered: room-temperature anodization at 0.2–0.5 mA/cm² in 0.1 M KOH / 90% EG / 10% water; Pt counter electrode; oxides to ~150 nm; 77-K C-V/interface-state data.

Disposition: P25/P25A electrode/material-state transfer evidence strengthened.

### R62-S06 — Nemirovsky and Kidron 1979 — `PT`
“The interface between Hg1-xCdxTe and its native oxide,” *Solid-State Electronics* 22 (1979) 831–837.

Recovered: 0.1 M KOH / 90% EG / 10% water; 0.5 mA/cm²; carbon counter electrode; galvanostatic formation followed by constant-voltage completion; 250–1250-Å oxide range; positive fixed charge.

Disposition: P25/P25A electrical termination mode strengthened; cell geometry remains `OPEN`.

### R62-S07 — Riedinger et al. 1992 — `PT`
“Chemimechanical polishing of cadmium telluride with bromine-methanol solutions,” *Mater. Sci. Eng. B* 15 (1992) L9–L12.

Recovered: Br2/methanol chemimechanical rate dependence; surface morphology versus Br concentration/pad contact; defined abrasive pre-removal.

Disposition: P29 surface-preparation mechanics transfer only.

### R62-S08 — Tobin et al. 1995 — `PT`
“The Relationship Between Lattice Matching and Crosshatch in Liquid Phase Epitaxy HgCdTe on CdZnTe Substrates,” *J. Electronic Materials* 24 (1995).

Recovered: crosshatch versus mismatch; near-perfect RT match ~+0.003%; crosshatch-free small-positive mismatch attributed to growth-temperature shift; EPD minimum ~1×10^4 cm^-2.

Disposition: P29 must distinguish `mismatch_RT` from `mismatch_Tgrowth`.

### R62-S09 — Everson et al. 1995 — `PT`
“Etch Pit Characterization of CdTe and CdZnTe Substrates for Use in Mercury Cadmium Telluride Epitaxy,” *J. Electronic Materials* 24 (1995).

Recovered B-face etch: `6 cm³ 48% HF + 24 cm³ HNO3 + 150 cm³ lactic acid` (`1:4:25`), room temperature, 2.5 min, moderate agitation; ~10:1 pit width:depth; validated `(111)B/(211)B` dislocation screen; example `EPD<1×10^5 cm^-2` high-quality-area screen.

Disposition: executable PT B-face EPD branch for P29.

### R62-S10 — Siliquini et al. 1997 — `SL`
“Scanning laser microscopy of reactive ion etching induced n-type conversion in vacancy-doped p-type HgCdTe,” *Appl. Phys. Lett.* 70, 3443 (1997), DOI `10.1063/1.119159`.

Recovered: Plasma Technology parallel plate; cathode 18 °C; H2 27 sccm; CH4 5 sccm; 410 mTorr; 0.4 W/cm²; printed dc bias 180 V; 60 s; `d_etch≈0.2 µm`; 1.047-µm LBIC; ~3-µm spot; 2.5-µm step; ~5-mm contact spacing; `d_conv≈1.5 µm`; best-fit converted-region net doping ~1×10^15 cm^-3.

Disposition: P34/P37 physical-state and depth-separation evidence strengthened; preserve the distinct Musca-1998 80-K LBIC branch.

### R62-S11 — Bartoli et al. 1976 — `PT`
“Thermal conductance of bonding layers in HgCdTe (PC) detector arrays,” *Applied Optics* 15 (1976) 2016–2017, DOI `10.1364/AO.15.002016`.

Recovered: 12-element Hg0.8Cd0.2Te PC array; 200-µm element; Irtran-2; 200-µs CO2 pulse at 16.5 W/cm²; epoxy `k/d≈3.2 W cm^-2 K^-1`; varnish `k/d≈0.9 W cm^-2 K^-1`; time-scale-separated thermal fitting.

Disposition: P33/P13 quantitative package-thermal prior added; values are not RP-01/Dow-3110 constants.

### R62-S12 — Stahle/Helms et al. 1989 — `PT`
“Physical and chemical properties of the anodic oxide/HgCdTe interface,” *JVST A* 7, 474 (1989), DOI `10.1116/1.576206`.

Recovered: x≈0.20–0.22; 0.1 M KOH / 90% EG / 10% water; 0.3 mA/cm²; ~700-Å oxide; ~30–50-Å CdTeO3-rich inner region plus a thicker mixed interface.

Disposition: P25 interface mechanism/current-density lineage strengthened.

### R62-S13 — Chandra, Schaake and Kinch 2003 — `PT`
“Low-Temperature Annealing of (Hg,Cd)Te,” *J. Electronic Materials* 32 (2003).

Recovered: Hg-saturated kinetics versus starting vacancy state, composition and temperature; skin depth ∝sqrt(t); `x_B²/t` rate coordinate; strong composition dependence; x≈0.28 activation energy ~1.1 eV; 225/270 °C study branches.

Disposition: P31/P23 kinetic model materially strengthened; no universal dwell created.

### R62-S14 — Bruder et al. 1990 — `PT`
“Vertical Bridgman growth of Cd1-yZnyTe and characterization of substrates for use in Hg1-xCdxTe liquid phase epitaxy,” *J. Crystal Growth* 101 (1990) 266–269.

Recovered: `(111)` orientation; XRD confirmation ~±0.3°; Br2/methanol chemimechanical finish; EPD/X-ray-topography/rocking-curve qualification.

Disposition: P29 incoming-substrate metrology lineage strengthened.

### R62-S15 — Tranchart et al. 1985 — `PT`
“LPE growth of Hg1-xCdxTe on Cd1-yZnyTe substrates,” *J. Crystal Growth* 72 (1985) 468–473.

Recovered: `y≈0.04` CdZnTe matching `x≈0.30` HgCdTe; Te-rich x≈0.30 LPE; 2% Br2/methanol pre-load etch; 3-mm liquid depth in that apparatus; `(111)A` process branch.

Disposition: P29 x≈0.30 lattice-match evidence strengthened; face/melt-depth transfer restrictions retained.

### R62-S16 — Radhakrishnan, Sitharaman and Gupta 2003 — `PT`
“Surface morphology of Hg0.8Cd0.2Te epilayers grown by LPE using horizontal slider,” *Applied Surface Science* 207 (2003) 33–39.

Recovered: `(111)B±0.5°` Cd0.96Zn0.04Te; 15×15-mm substrate; 10×10-mm layer; 20–40 µm; 480–460 °C; 0.5 °C/min; wipe/slider-scratch mechanisms; branch-specific >~350 °C post-separation Hg-evaporation observation and low-temperature HgTe-soot observation.

Disposition: P30 wipe/post-separation state strengthened; ~350 °C is not universal.

### R62-S17 — Parker, Weirauch and Chandra 1988 — `PT`
“Terracing in HgCdTe LPE films grown from Te solution,” *J. Crystal Growth* 86 (1988) 173–182.

Recovered: terracing versus holder design, stirring/rotation, meltback, thermal gradient and growth rate; obstruction-driven hydrodynamics; low-obstruction holder reduced terracing.

Disposition: P30 geometry/hydrodynamics rationale strengthened; non-slider crucible dimensions not transferred.

### R62-S18 — Chen et al. 1991 — `PT`
“Growth of Hg1-xCdxTe heterolayers by slider LPE using separate compensating atmosphere of mercury,” *J. Crystal Growth* (1991) 520–526.

Recovered: three-zone furnace; TAC + three-chamber graphite boat; separate reservoir temperatures; 500/450 °C homogenization/saturation; 5–17 °C supercooling branches; postgrowth Hg-vapor-protected cooldown.

Disposition: P30 independently programmed Hg-chemical-potential boundary strengthened.

### R62-S19 — Astles et al. 1992 — `PT`
“Improved control of composition and electrical properties of liquid phase epitaxial (CdHg)Te layers,” *J. Crystal Growth* 117 (1992) 213–217.

Recovered: POCO DFP-2 graphite; acid etch; one-week boiling-DI clean; 500 °C bake; scratch-free hand-polished mating faces; shimmed slider rails; dual HgTe-source architecture; ~0.3 mg/min Hg loss; ~460 °C / 0.1 °C min^-1 growth; 250 °C / 48 h Hg-rich anneal example.

Disposition: G61-04/P30 graphite-cleaning restriction refined; P31 transfer prior broadened.
